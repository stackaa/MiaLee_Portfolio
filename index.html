About me

I’m Mia, a UGC creator specialising in beauty, hair and lifestyle content for modern digital brands.

My work combines refined visuals with a strong understanding of paid media and platform-native storytelling. In addition to campaign-based collaborations, I support brands through ongoing creative partnerships — identifying content opportunities, seasonal moments and strategic angles that drive consistent, performance-led output.

Over the past years, I’ve collaborated with brands across beauty, fashion, tech and travel, delivering both structured campaign creative and long-term content development.

I create primarily in English, with French-language delivery available for EU-based campaigns.
